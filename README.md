Status: Submitted to The Visual Computer. 
Abstract Virtual try-on (VTON) systems are pivotal for online fashion platforms, requiring both realistic garment visualization and efficient inference. Traditional methods often rely on explicit human representations, leading to high computational cost and limited practicality. We propose LEC-VTON, a parser-free teacher–student framework that achieves high-fidelity generation with efficiency. The teacher network, ContextEdgeNet, leverages context-aware deformation modeling and edge-guided feature fusion for precise garment alignment and texture preservation. Through knowledge distillation, these capabilities are transferred to a lightweight student network, LiteFitNet, enabling direct generation of try-on images from human and garment inputs. Experiments on VITON and VITON-HD demonstrate that LEC-VTON maintains a lightweight design while achieving strong visual quality, even under challenging poses and complex textures (LPIPS: 0.084 / 0.092).
